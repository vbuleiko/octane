Questions

Do I need my own domain?

No. Your website works on our address straight away. If you have a domain, like joesmotors.co.za, you add one record in its settings and your website opens there — we'll send you the steps.

Will my cars go on AutoTrader by themselves?

Facebook and Instagram: yes, you connect your catalogue yourself in a few minutes. AutoTrader, Cars.co.za, Gumtree and AutoMart take automatic stock only from providers they approve: tap Connect in the app and we arrange it for your account. Until then, “Copy ad” and “Download photos” put a car on any portal in a minute.

I already have a website.

Keep it and link it to your stock page — or point your domain to us and get the whole website with your cars always up to date.

Is it fast on a phone?

Yes. Pages are light, every photo comes in the size the screen needs, and nothing moves around while it loads. Buyers on 4G see your cars in about a second.

Is my data safe?

Your cars and leads are only visible to you. We never sell data or contact your buyers. Registration numbers and what you paid for a car are never shown. See our privacy policy.

How do I pay?

By EFT after your free trial — {{ config.PRICE_MONTHLY }} a month, or {{ config.PRICE_YEARLY }} for a year upfront. We'll remind you a few days before the trial ends.
